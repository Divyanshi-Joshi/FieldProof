────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 — INDEX TRIGGER AND PAYOUT DETERMINATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-1.1  STANDARD RAINFALL INDEX TRIGGER (30TH PERCENTILE)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The Index shall trigger an automatic payout when cumulative seasonal
  rainfall recorded at the Insured Plot falls strictly below the 30th
  percentile of the long-term historical rainfall distribution for the
  relevant county and season window.

  The baseline distribution shall be derived from ERA5 reanalysis data
  covering the 30-year climatological reference period 1991–2020, obtained
  via the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, or from the multi-season
  county-level empirical distribution in the contracted dataset when live
  API access is unavailable.

  Formally:
    index_pays = TRUE when season_rain_mm < trigger_rain_mm
  where trigger_rain_mm is the precipitation depth (mm) at the 30th
  percentile (trigger_percentile = 30) of the baseline distribution.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 — CONFIRMED PAYOUT AND OVERPAYMENT REVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-2.1  CONFIRMED PAYOUT FAST-TRACK
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where the Index triggers (index_pays = TRUE) and independent
  photographic evidence confirms crop damage at MODERATE or SEVERE
  severity, the claim shall be fast-tracked for adjuster sign-off
  without further evidence gathering.

  CL-2.2  POSSIBLE OVERPAYMENT REVIEW
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where the Index triggers (index_pays = TRUE) but photographic evidence
  does NOT confirm MODERATE or SEVERE crop damage, the claim shall be
  flagged for manual review by a qualified loss adjuster to assess
  whether the index payout is warranted despite the absence of visible
  field-level damage.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 — PHOTO EVIDENCE ASSESSMENT AND CALIBRATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-3.1  OBSERVABLE SIGN RUBRIC
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Photo evidence shall be assessed using a concept-bottleneck rubric
  scoring four observable indicators: leaf rolling (0–3), canopy
  senescence (0–3), stunting (0–3), and visible soil cracking (boolean).
  Severity bands (NONE, MILD, MODERATE, SEVERE) shall be determined by
  deterministic tested code, not by the perception model.

  CL-3.2  VISUAL ANCHOR CALIBRATION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Each assessment shall include three reference photographs (healthy,
  moderate, severe) from the development set, excluded from any
  evaluation test set, to calibrate model perception.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 — BASIS-RISK ESCALATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-4.1  BASIS-RISK DEFINITION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Basis risk arises when the area-level index does not trigger yet
  individual plot-level evidence confirms crop loss. The Insurer shall
  maintain a reconciliation process to detect, classify, and escalate
  such gaps.

  CL-4.2  PHENOLOGICAL BASIS-RISK ESCALATION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where the Index does NOT trigger, but (a) photographic evidence
  confirms MODERATE or SEVERE drought damage, (b) satellite vegetation
  anomaly confirms stress (NDVI anomaly z-score < −1.0), and (c) a
  continuous dry spell overlaps the crop's critical reproductive or
  flowering growth stage, the claim shall be classified as a
  PHENOLOGICAL BASIS-RISK GAP and escalated for adjuster review.

  CL-4.3  SPATIAL BASIS-RISK ESCALATION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where the Index does NOT trigger, but (a) photographic evidence
  confirms MODERATE or SEVERE drought damage, (b) satellite vegetation
  anomaly confirms stress, and (c) the dry spell does NOT overlap the
  critical growth stage but plot-level seasonal rainfall is below the
  county median (low_local_rain = TRUE), the claim shall be classified
  as a SPATIAL BASIS-RISK GAP and escalated for adjuster review.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 — EVIDENCE CONFLICT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-5.1  EVIDENCE CONFLICT HOLD
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where the Index does NOT trigger, photographic evidence confirms
  MODERATE or SEVERE weather damage, but satellite vegetation data does
  NOT confirm stress, the claim shall be placed on hold. The system
  shall request additional photographs from the policyholder and route
  the claim for human expert review.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 — EXCLUSIONS AND NON-WEATHER PERILS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-6.1  NON-WEATHER PERIL EXCLUSION AND ADVISORY ROUTING
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  This policy covers drought-related crop loss only. Where photographic
  evidence identifies damage caused by pests, disease, weeds, nutrient
  deficiency, or other non-weather perils, the claim shall NOT be
  processed under this index policy. The system shall route the claim
  to the appropriate agricultural advisory service or alternative
  coverage product.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 — HUMAN OVERSIGHT AND ADJUSTER AUTHORITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-7.1  MANDATORY HUMAN ADJUSTER SIGN-OFF AND TWO-PASS ABSTENTION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  No payout shall be issued without explicit sign-off by a qualified
  human loss adjuster. The automated system provides recommendations
  only; the adjuster retains sole authority to approve, reject, or
  escalate any claim.

  Where the two independent photo-assessment passes produce different
  severity bands (passes_agreed = FALSE), the system shall abstain from
  making any automated recommendation. The claim shall be immediately
  routed to a human adjuster for manual review with full photo evidence,
  sign scores from both passes, and all supporting data.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 — IMAGE INTEGRITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CL-8.1  IMAGE INTEGRITY HOLD
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Where any uploaded photograph fails integrity checks — including but
  not limited to perceptual-hash duplicate detection (within the claim
  bundle or across prior claims), screen-recapture detection, mismatched
  fields across photographs, or missing/inconsistent EXIF metadata —
  the claim shall be placed on INTEGRITY HOLD. No automated payout
  recommendation shall be made. The claim shall be referred to the
  fraud and operations team for investigation.
